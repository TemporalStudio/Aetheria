## 📖 INSPIRATION & ACKNOWLEDGMENT

AETHERIA draws inspiration from the Korean fantasy web novel:

**Pick Me Up! (Pick Me Up, Infinite Gacha)**

- Original Author: **Hermod**
- Original Language: Korean
- Genre: Action, Fantasy, Adventure

The novel inspired our interest in game-system mechanics, hero management, character progression, and survival-oriented challenges.

AETHERIA is an independently developed project and is not an official adaptation, collaboration, or affiliated product of the original novel.

All original characters, assets, artwork, music, and intellectual property belong to their respective owners.
